Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-05.

Feral has one user: its owner. It only signs in to the owner's own Google account and does not access anyone else's account.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it can read, label, draft and send Gmail messages, change Gmail settings, and read and change Google Calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used only to give the owner these features. Sign-in credentials are stored on the owner's own computer.

Who receives it

When the owner asks an AI assistant to work with their mail or calendar, the text involved is sent to the AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the answer. The data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone else.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
